What is Pranali?

Pranali is a state-of-the-art Integrated Financial Management System (IFMS) developed for the Government of Sikkim. It serves as a single source of truth for all financial transactions, budgeting, and employee records.

Core Objectives

Designed to enhance transparency and accountability in government spending. Pranali automates complex financial workflows, reducing manual errors and ensuring real-time auditing.

Employee Empowerment

Through this portal, employees can access digital services such as Payslip generation, Leave management, and Personal Profile updates with unprecedented ease.

Driving Digital Transformation

Pranali is more than just a software; it's a commitment to a Digital Sikkim. By digitizing the treasury and financial processes, we are building a more responsive and resilient governance model.
